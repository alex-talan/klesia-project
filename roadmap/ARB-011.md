## Questions

1. Operating system (Linux preferred), pricing tier, region.
2. Deployment slots available (staging slot + swap) or not.
3. Instance count and autoscale rule.
4. Health check thresholds and the accepted platform reaction to a long database outage (all instances failing `/health`).

## Recommendation

Linux, a tier with Always On and slots, at least 2 instances in production, Health check enabled with the DSI thresholds.
