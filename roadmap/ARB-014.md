## Context

- DEC-24: one **private** container, anonymous access disabled, **shared-key access disabled**, no CORS, no SAS; the API is the only reader (managed identity, *Storage Blob Data Contributor*).
- OPEN-12c: redundancy, soft delete, retention and lifecycle of the blobs; one account per environment. OPEN-12d: load of the PDFs on the App Service.
- Nothing deletes demands, so blobs live as long as their demand unless a retention policy is decided (see ARB-020).

## Questions

1. Redundancy (LRS, ZRS, GRS…).
2. Blob soft delete / versioning and their retention.
3. Lifecycle management (tiering, deletion) — must stay consistent with ARB-020 and with the fact that `bae_link` never expires.
4. One account per environment (confirm) and container name.

## Recommendation

ZRS, blob soft delete 30 days, no automatic deletion until ARB-020 is settled, one account per environment, container `bae`.
